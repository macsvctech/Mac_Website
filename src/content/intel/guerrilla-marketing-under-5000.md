## 01_ASYMMETRIC_TACTICS: THE ART OF UNINVITED ATTENTION

When marketing budgets are restricted to petty cash, conventional advertising is financial suicide. You cannot outspend corporate algorithms on Google or Meta; you must out-think physical space.

Guerrilla marketing is the deliberate exploitation of environmental context to deliver a commercial message that cannot be skipped, scrolled past, or blocked with an ad-blocker.

> "A CAN OF WASHABLE CHALK PAINT AND A CLEVER STENCIL WILL OUTPERFORM A 50,000 RUPEE INSTAGRAM AD CAMPAIGN EVERY SINGLE TIME."
> 
> — K. NAIR, CREATIVE DIRECTIVES

## 02_PROVEN_LOW_BUDGET_EXECUTIONS

In this video briefing and case analysis, we review three distinct stunts staged on Delhi University campuses:
1. **The 'Abandoned Briefcase' Paradox:** Triggering curiosity loops without creating public panic.
2. **Reverse Graffiti:** Cleaning sidewalk grime through a laser-cut stencil to leave a sparkling brand message without vandalism citations.
3. **The Counter-Intuitive Caution Tape:** Wrapping ordinary benches and bulletin boards with faux caution warnings to force 100% stop-and-stare eye contact.
