## 01_NEUROLOGICAL_COMPRESSION: THE REWIRING OF PATIENCE

The human brain did not evolve to anticipate grocery deliveries within 600 seconds. When consumer platforms collapsed the latency between desire and physical delivery from three days to ten minutes, they triggered a fundamental neurochemical feedback loop.

Quick commerce is not a grocery business; it is an impulse gratification infrastructure. By placing 2,500 SKU dark stores within 1.8 kilometers of every high-density residential pin code in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and Bengaluru, operators transformed the physical city into a distributed warehouse.

## 02_UNIT_ECONOMICS: THE DARK STORE ANATOMY

Behind the polished consumer app interface sits a brutal operational reality:
1. **Picker Route Optimization:** Algorithmic pick-paths that shave 8 seconds off every SKU collection.
2. **Dense Route Clustering:** Riders completing 3.4 drops per hour during peak evening traffic windows.
3. **High-Margin Basket Bundling:** Cross-selling ice cream, electronics cables, and energy drinks with 40% margins alongside zero-margin milk.
