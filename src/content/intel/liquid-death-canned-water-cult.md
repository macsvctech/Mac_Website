## 01_COMMODITY_SUBVERSION: MURDERING THIRST

Water is universally available from municipal taps for fractions of a penny. For three decades, corporate water giants differentiated their products through pseudo-medical purity claims: pristine alpine glaciers, volcanic basalt filtration, and pH balances.

Liquid Death recognized that health claims are boring. By packaging mountain water in 500ml tallboy cans adorned with gothic skulls, heavy metal typography, and the slogan 'Murder Your Thirst', they turned hydration into an irreverent performance art piece.

> "IF YOU CAN MAKE WATER LOOK LIKE A PUNK ROCK ALBUM, YOU CAN SELL ANY COMMODITY AT A 400% LUXURY MARKUP."
> 
> — A. VERMA, MAC BRAND STRATEGY

## 02_ORGANIC_VIRALITY_MECHANICS

Rather than spending millions on corporate endorsements, the brand:
1. Sells out-of-home merchandise that fans willingly pay to advertise.
2. Creates self-deprecating video skits that mock corporate advertising tropes.
3. Treats corporate haters and negative review comments as lyrics for punk rock vinyl albums.
