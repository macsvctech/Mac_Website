## 01_NEURAL_TELEMETRY: BEYOND SELF-REPORTED SURVEYS

Traditional focus groups are fundamentally broken: consumers lie to appear rational, socially conscious, and financially prudent. When evaluating streetwear and apparel purchases, what shoppers say they value bears almost zero statistical correlation with actual purchasing behavior.

Using portable eye-tracking apparatus and galvanic skin response sensors across 120 collegiate subjects in South Delhi retail hubs, we measured genuine autonomic responses to packaging, lighting, and garment placement.

> "CONSUMERS DO NOT BUY CLOTHES TO COVER THEMSELVES; THEY PURCHASE EMBEDDED CULTURAL BADGES TO SIGNAL TRIBAL AFFILIATION IN REAL-TIME."
> 
> — R. SHARMA, MAC RESEARCH DEPT

## 02_RETAIL_ENVIRONMENT_TACTICS

Key observations for retail merchandisers:
- **Spatial Tension:** Minimalist rack spacing that creates artificial scarcity prompts higher touch frequency.
- **Auditory Priming:** Industrial low-fi beats maintain calm analytical evaluation while upbeat tempos drive hurried checkout.
